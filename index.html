Japan visa forms, made easy

Fill in your details once, then download Excel or PDF in the same item order as the official Change of Status of Residence or Extension of Period of Stay form (applicant, part 1).

1

Download the official form

Always use the latest official file from the Immigration Services Agency (ISA). The form differs by status of residence (Student, Dependent, Engineer/Specialist, etc.), so pick the one for your status.

  • Change of Status of Residence – applicant, part 1 (official PDF, Form 30)
  • Extension of Period of Stay – all forms (English page)
  • Extension of Period of Stay – all forms (Japanese page)
  • Procedure guide and required documents (multilingual)
  • Forms for the "Student" status

For Student to work visa, you also need the part 2 form for your new job type and the employer's forms. Find them on the ISA site (the English page above lists forms by status). Each ISA page offers the form as PDF and Excel. Apply before your period of stay expires (from 3 months before).

2

Fill in your details

Write in English letters as shown in your passport. Your entries are saved automatically on this device.

3

Download your data

Tip: for PDF choose "Save as PDF" in the print window. Use "Save my data" to keep a backup or move to another device, and share the site link with friends: each person's data stays on their own device.

Unofficial helper tool. It does not replace the official form: copy the values into the ISA form and check them against the current ISA instructions before submitting.
